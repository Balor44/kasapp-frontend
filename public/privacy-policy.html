Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 28, 2026

KASAPP TECHNOLOGIES ("Kasapp", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you access or use the Kasapp conversational interface via WhatsApp, our website, and associated Web3 utility services.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our WhatsApp-based wallet and utility payment services, we collect and process the following categories of information:

  • Contact & Identifier Data: Your WhatsApp phone number, WhatsApp profile name, and user ID.
  • Security & Authentication Data: Hashed transaction PINs (we store only irreversible cryptographic hashes, never plaintext PINs).
  • Utility & Payment Details: Meter numbers, smartcard/IUC numbers, and destination phone numbers provided for airtime, data, electricity, or cable TV purchases.
  • Blockchain & Transaction Data: Public Kaspa wallet addresses, transaction hashes (TXIDs), voucher redemption codes, and utility settlement reference IDs.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Service Delivery: Generating your secure Kaspa wallet and executing on-chain transfers.
  • Utility Payments: Facilitating airtime top-ups, data bundles, electricity tokens, and cable TV subscriptions.
  • Security: Verifying transaction PINs and preventing unauthorized access.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with necessary third-party service providers:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc.: To transmit encrypted messages via the WhatsApp Cloud API.
  • Utility Aggregators (e.g., VTpass): To process telecommunications and electricity services.
  • Public Blockchain Networks: Sender/recipient addresses and TXIDs are permanently broadcast to the public Kaspa BlockDAG by design.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

You have the right to request the deletion of your personal data stored on our servers. To request account deletion:

  1. Send a WhatsApp message with the keyword "Delete My Account" to the Kasapp bot, or
  2. Send an email to admin@mykasapp.com with your registered phone number.

Note: Records already broadcast to the public Kaspa blockchain cannot be altered or deleted due to the immutable nature of distributed ledger technology.
